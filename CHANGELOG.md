The first public portfolio release will include authentication and RBAC, multi-company master data, transactional stock and physical inventory, purchasing and receiving, sales reservation and shipment, manual finance, dashboards, reports, audit records, security hardening, CI/CD, and public deployment.

The version remains planned until the finalization branch is integrated and production is revalidated.
